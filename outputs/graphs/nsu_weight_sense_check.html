NSU Weight Sense-Check

Mean grams per non-standard unit from outcome2_lookup, by municipality and price rung, one panel per harmonized unit. Western Visayas market survey, five provinces.

cheapest rung middle dearest rung no weight behind the point — refused, not imputed dotted baseline = fewer than 3 weighings axis under each bar = its price; shading follows the same order

Each bar is w_g, the median market-survey weight behind one price point; the label under it names the rung, and the numeral above it is that weight. A dotted baseline marks n_g < 3 — the project's THIN threshold, where the value is published but flagged. Hover or tab to any bar for the full rung name, the price, n_g, and how many of those weighings were questioned. A hatched ghost bar is a price point the lookup carries with no weight behind it: a household matching it is refused rather than converted, which is why the point is kept rather than deleted.

The axis under each bar is that bar's price point, in pesos. A rung is a price point, the price is what orders the bars, and it is the one label that means the same thing on all three branches. What kind of point it is — mp25, a municipal or province median, a unique municipal price, or the second of three cuts on the size-based branch — is written out in the tooltip, where there is room for it.

Under each panel heading is the raw pull_nsu_unit spelling, or spellings, that fold into it. A harmonized unit is a pooling decision, not a field response: pieces or units is bilog in one municipality and binilog in another, and whether those name the same object is a judgement the chart cannot show on its own. A spelling marked priced, not weighed appears in the price file for that case but was never weighed there — it still shapes the case, because a household reporting it is scored against a ladder built from the other spelling's prices. The tooltip and the table give the spellings for one municipality at a time; the heading gives the union across every municipality drawn.

That distinction matters because the two branches build their rungs from opposite directions. On the price-quantity branch the enumerator was sent to a named price point and weighed what the vendor handed over, so the rung is the price file's own label. On the size-based branch nothing was weighed against a price at all: the weights were pooled across sizes and cut into as many parts as the case had convertible price points, cheapest weights to the cheapest price.
